# Licensing

This repository carries two licences, because it holds two different kinds of material.

## Factual data, CC0 1.0 Universal

The structured factual fields are released into the public domain under CC0 1.0 — see LICENSE. No permission is needed and no attribution is required.

This covers: who performed on a recording and on what instrument; dates, places, labels, catalogue and matrix numbers; band membership and its dates; birth and death dates and places; and the source identifiers attached to any of these.

These are facts. In the United States facts are not copyrightable, and we do not claim rights we do not hold. CC0 also means the data can be absorbed by Wikidata, MusicBrainz and other open projects, which is the point of releasing it.

## Editorial content, CC BY 4.0

The following are original creative work, licensed under CC BY 4.0 — any use permitted, with attribution. Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

- Free-text notes, descriptions and commentary on any record
- The relationship vocabulary, and the judgments made with it, including which ensembles are treated as apprenticeship schools
- Confidence assessments and source-tier assignments
- The schema and its documentation

The schema marks which fields are editorial.

## Citing this dataset

Attribution is not legally required for the factual layer. It is welcome, and it is how this work continues. Please cite the dataset by its DOI.

## Third-party sources

Facts here are compiled from sources that carry their own terms, recorded per claim in the source registry. Compiling them here does not relicense them. Consult the original source before reusing its material.
